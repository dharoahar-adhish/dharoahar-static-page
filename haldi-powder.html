Haldi Powder

✨ Pure Turmeric

Origin: Tamil Nadu & Maharashtra

Dharoahar Haldi Powder is made from selected turmeric, finely ground to deliver a vibrant golden colour, warm aroma and characteristic turmeric flavour. An essential ingredient in Indian kitchens, haldi forms the foundation of countless traditional recipes.

Its rich golden colour makes it ideal for curries, dals, vegetables, gravies and rice preparations, while its warm flavour blends naturally with other spices. The fine powder mixes easily into cooking, helping distribute colour and flavour evenly throughout a dish.

Haldi Powder is also a versatile ingredient for marinades, spice blends and everyday seasoning. From simple home-cooked meals to elaborate traditional preparations, it brings the familiar colour and character that define many Indian dishes.
