Degi Mirch

✨ Mild Heat. Vibrant Colour.

Origin: Andhra Pradesh

Dharoahar Degi Mirch is a finely ground red chilli powder selected for its vibrant colour, smooth texture and balanced level of heat. It is an ideal choice when you want to bring rich colour and a gentle warmth to a dish without overpowering its natural flavours.

Degi Mirch is a versatile ingredient in Indian cooking. Its bright red colour makes it particularly suitable for curries, gravies, marinades and traditional preparations where visual appeal is just as important as flavour. The powder blends easily with other spices and ingredients, creating a rich, consistent finish.

From everyday home cooking to restaurant-style preparations, Degi Mirch adds a warm red hue and mild chilli character to recipes. Its fine texture makes it convenient to incorporate directly into spice mixes, marinades, sauces and gravies.
