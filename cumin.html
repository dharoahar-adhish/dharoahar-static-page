Cumin

🌿 Earthy Warmth

Origin: Rajasthan

Dharoahar Cumin is selected for its distinctive earthy aroma, warm flavour and characteristic nuttiness. An essential whole spice in Indian kitchens, cumin brings a familiar depth and fragrance to everyday cooking.

Cumin seeds are especially valued for tempering and tadka, where their aroma is released in hot oil or ghee before other ingredients are added. They can also be used whole in rice preparations, curries, dals, vegetables, gravies and a wide variety of traditional recipes.

Beyond tempering, whole cumin can be roasted to deepen its flavour or incorporated into homemade spice blends. Its warm, earthy character pairs naturally with other Indian spices, making it a versatile ingredient for both everyday meals and more elaborate preparations.
