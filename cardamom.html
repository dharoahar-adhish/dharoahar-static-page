Cardamom

🌿 Queen of Spices

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Green Cardamom is valued for its distinctive fragrance, delicate sweetness and refreshing aromatic character. Known as the Queen of Spices, cardamom brings an elegant layer of flavour to both traditional Indian recipes and contemporary cuisine.

The whole green pods are ideal for preparations where their natural aroma can gradually infuse into the dish. They can be used in rice dishes, curries, gravies, desserts and beverages, or incorporated into homemade spice blends for a more complex aromatic profile.

Cardamom is equally at home in sweet and savoury cooking. Its naturally fragrant profile pairs beautifully with other warm spices, making it an essential ingredient for festive preparations, traditional recipes and everyday culinary creations.

  • Origin: Kerala
  • Distinctive and refreshing aroma
  • Delicate natural sweetness
  • Suitable for sweet and savoury preparations
  • Ideal for beverages and desserts
  • Excellent for traditional spice blends
