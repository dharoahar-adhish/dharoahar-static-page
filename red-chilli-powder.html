Red Chilli Powder (लाल मिर्च)

🌶️ Bold Colour & Chilli Character

Red Chilli Powder is a staple of Indian cooking, bringing characteristic chilli flavour, colour and warmth to a wide variety of recipes. Its finely ground format makes it easy to use across everyday cooking.

Dharoahar Red Chilli Powder is suited to curries, gravies, marinades, spice blends and a variety of regional Indian preparations. It can be used as part of the cooking process or added according to the desired flavour profile of a dish.

From everyday dal and sabzi preparations to richer gravies and masalas, red chilli powder is an essential ingredient for building the character of Indian cuisine.
