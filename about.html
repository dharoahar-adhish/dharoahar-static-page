About Dharoahar

Building a spice business around trust.

Dharoahar brings together Indian whole spices, powders and traditional spice products with a focus on thoughtful sourcing, documented quality and dependable supply.

Our Story

Indian spices, built into a dependable business.

Dharoahar was created with a simple objective: to build a dependable business around Indian spices.

We bring together whole spices and powdered spices from established sourcing regions across India, with a focus on product selection, documented quality and dependable supply.

Our portfolio is being built for both consumers and businesses — from everyday Indian kitchens to retailers, distributors, food businesses and commercial buyers.
