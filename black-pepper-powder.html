Black Pepper Powder (काली मिर्च)

🌿 Bold & Peppery

Black Pepper Powder is a finely ground form of one of the most familiar spices in Indian kitchens. Known for its distinctive aroma and characteristic peppery flavour, it adds warmth and depth to a wide range of everyday preparations.

Dharoahar Black Pepper Powder is suited to both traditional Indian cooking and contemporary food preparations. Its convenient powdered form makes it easy to incorporate into marinades, gravies, soups, sauces, seasonings and spice blends.

From a finishing touch on a prepared dish to an ingredient in a carefully balanced recipe, black pepper brings a distinctive flavour that works across cuisines.
