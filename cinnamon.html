Cinnamon

🌿 Sweet Warmth

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Cinnamon is selected for its naturally warm aroma, delicate sweetness and subtle spicy character. A versatile whole spice, cinnamon adds depth and fragrance to both traditional Indian recipes and contemporary cooking.

Carefully selected cinnamon bark brings a balanced flavour that works equally well in sweet and savoury preparations. It can be used whole while cooking rice, curries, gravies and slow-cooked dishes, or added to homemade spice blends for a richer aromatic profile.

Cinnamon is valued not only for its distinctive flavour but also for the warmth and complexity it brings to a dish. From everyday Indian cooking to festive recipes and aromatic beverages, it is a dependable kitchen essential for creating layered flavour.
