About Cinnamon

A versatile whole spice for Indian kitchens.

Cinnamon, known as Dalchini in Hindi, is a familiar whole spice used in both sweet and savoury Indian preparations for its distinctive aroma and warm flavour.

Dharoahar Cinnamon is sourced from Kerala and can be used in everyday cooking as well as traditional recipes, including rice dishes, curries, beverages and desserts.

In the Kitchen

Where Cinnamon fits in.

01

Rice Dishes

Commonly used in biryani, pulao and other aromatic rice preparations.

02

Adds warmth and aroma to traditional curries, gravies and slow-cooked dishes.

03

Beverages

Used in tea, spiced drinks and other warm beverage preparations.

Works well in selected Indian sweets and dessert preparations.
