Garam Masala

✨ Spice Blend

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Garam Masala is a carefully balanced blend of selected spices created to bring warmth, depth and aromatic complexity to Indian cooking. A staple of traditional kitchens, it adds a rich finishing layer to a wide variety of everyday and celebratory dishes.

Its warm and aromatic character makes it especially suitable for curries, gravies, dals and slow-cooked preparations. Garam Masala is typically added towards the later stages of cooking or used as a finishing spice, allowing its layered fragrance to remain prominent in the finished dish.

Versatile and easy to use, it can also be incorporated into marinades, vegetable preparations, rice dishes and homemade seasoning mixes. A small addition can bring together the flavours of a dish while adding the characteristic warmth associated with classic Indian spice blends.
