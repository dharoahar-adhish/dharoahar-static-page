Fenugreek Seeds (मेथी)

🌿 Warm & Distinctive

Fenugreek Seeds, traditionally known as Methi, are small, golden-brown seeds valued for their distinctive aroma and characteristic slightly bitter, warm flavour. A familiar ingredient in Indian kitchens, they add depth and complexity to everyday cooking.

Dharoahar Fenugreek Seeds are suited to traditional Indian cooking across dals, vegetables, pickles, spice blends and regional preparations. Used whole or lightly roasted, fenugreek seeds can bring a distinctive layer of flavour to a dish without overpowering its other ingredients.

From a simple tempering to a carefully prepared masala blend, Methi remains an important whole spice in Indian culinary traditions.

CULINARY USE

How Fenugreek Seeds Are Used

A versatile whole spice used across everyday Indian cooking and traditional preparations.

Tempering

Add whole fenugreek seeds to hot oil as part of a traditional tempering for dals, vegetables and regional preparations.

Pickles

Fenugreek seeds are commonly used in Indian pickles, where their distinctive flavour complements other spices.

Spice Blends

Use in traditional masala blends to add depth and a warm, characteristic spice note.

Everyday Cooking

Suitable for selected dal, vegetable and regional recipes where a distinctive whole-spice flavour is desired.

STORAGE

Store for Freshness

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep the product in a clean, dry and tightly closed container after opening.
