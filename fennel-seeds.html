Fennel Seeds

सौंफ

🌿 Naturally Sweet & Aromatic

Dharoahar Fennel Seeds, traditionally known as Saunf, are valued for their naturally sweet aroma, refreshing character and distinctive flavour. A familiar ingredient in Indian kitchens, fennel adds a gentle aromatic note to both traditional recipes and contemporary culinary preparations.

Whole fennel seeds can be lightly roasted to bring out their natural fragrance or used directly while cooking. They work beautifully in spice blends, vegetable preparations, curries, pickles and selected rice dishes, where their subtle sweetness can complement other spices.

Fennel is also commonly enjoyed as a traditional after-meal spice. Its fresh, aromatic character makes it a versatile whole spice that can be used in everyday cooking as well as in traditional Indian culinary practices.

With its delicate sweetness and distinctive aroma, Saunf pairs naturally with other whole spices and adds another layer of flavour to carefully prepared spice blends and dishes.
