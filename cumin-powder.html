Cumin Powder

✨ Fine Ground

Origin: Rajasthan

Dharoahar Cumin Powder is made from premium cumin seeds sourced from Rajasthan and finely ground to create a smooth, ready-to-use spice. It brings the distinctive earthy aroma, warm character and authentic flavour of cumin to everyday Indian cooking.

Cumin Powder is a versatile kitchen essential, adding depth and fragrance to curries, dals, gravies, rice preparations and vegetable dishes. Its fine texture makes it easy to blend evenly with other ingredients, helping create a consistent flavour throughout the dish.

It can also be used in marinades, spice mixes, raitas and traditional preparations where the characteristic warmth of cumin is an important part of the flavour profile. Whether used as a primary seasoning or combined with other spices, it adds a familiar earthy note to a wide range of recipes.
