Bay Leaf

तेज पत्ता

🌿 Fragrant & Aromatic

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Bay Leaf, traditionally known as Tej Patta, is a classic whole spice valued for its distinctive fragrance and subtle depth of flavour. It is widely used in Indian cooking to build a warm and aromatic foundation in rice dishes, curries, gravies and slow-cooked preparations.

Whole bay leaves are generally added during cooking and removed before serving. Their natural aroma gradually infuses into the dish, complementing other whole spices and ingredients without overpowering the overall flavour.

Bay Leaf is particularly suited to biryani, pulao, dals, curries, gravies and traditional spice preparations. It also works well alongside spices such as cumin, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves, making it an important part of many classic Indian spice combinations.

Its versatility makes Tej Patta a useful everyday whole spice, whether used in home cooking, traditional recipes or more elaborate preparations where layered aroma and depth are important.
