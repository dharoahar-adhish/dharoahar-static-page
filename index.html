The Dharoahar Spice Signature

Four regions. Four ways of building flavour.

Explore Indian flavour through four broad regional traditions.

North India

North

Warming whole spices, wheat-based staples, slow cooking and rich gravies create a familiar North Indian flavour vocabulary.

East India

East

Mustard, subtle spice combinations, rice-led meals and distinctive regional preparations shape the eastern table.

West India

West

Bold tempering, layered textures, tangy flavours and diverse vegetarian traditions define much of western India.

South India

South

Rice, lentils, tempering spices, coconut and regional chilli profiles create a remarkably diverse southern table.
