About the Spice

A warm and aromatic kitchen staple.

Ginger powder, commonly known as sonth, is a ground spice used across Indian culinary preparations. Its warm and aromatic character makes it suitable for both everyday cooking and traditional recipes.

It can be used in spice blends, curries, marinades and beverages, and is also incorporated into a variety of regional Indian preparations.
