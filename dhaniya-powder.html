Dhaniya Powder

✨ Coriander

Origin: Rajasthan

Dharoahar Dhaniya Powder is made from premium coriander seeds sourced from Rajasthan and finely ground to deliver a smooth texture and naturally aromatic flavour. A foundational spice in Indian kitchens, coriander powder adds depth, balance and fragrance to a wide range of everyday dishes.

Its mild, warm and naturally aromatic character makes it an essential ingredient for curries, gravies, dals, vegetables and traditional Indian preparations. The fine powder blends easily with other spices, helping create a balanced and consistent flavour throughout the dish.

Dhaniya Powder is equally useful in homemade spice blends, marinades and seasoning mixes. Used on its own or alongside cumin, chilli and other traditional spices, it provides a distinctive coriander note that forms the foundation of many classic Indian flavour profiles.
