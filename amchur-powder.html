Amchur Powder (अमचूर)

🍋 Tangy & Distinctive

Amchur Powder, traditionally known as Amchur, is a finely ground spice made from dried raw mango. It is valued in Indian cooking for its characteristic tangy flavour and is widely used to bring a bright, sharp note to savoury preparations.

Dharoahar Amchur Powder is suited to a wide range of Indian recipes, including vegetable preparations, chutneys, marinades, chaats and spice blends. Its powdered format makes it convenient to use while cooking or as part of a finishing seasoning.

A small amount can add a distinctive tangy character to dishes, making Amchur a useful ingredient in everyday Indian kitchens as well as regional culinary preparations.
