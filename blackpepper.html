Black Pepper

🌿 King of Spices

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Black Pepper is selected for its bold aroma, distinctive warmth and characteristic peppery heat. Known as the King of Spices, black pepper is one of the most versatile ingredients in the kitchen, bringing depth and character to everyday cooking.

Our whole peppercorns are ideal for recipes where the spice is allowed to slowly release its flavour during cooking. They can be used in tempering, gravies, curries, rice preparations, marinades, soups and broths, or freshly ground whenever a more pronounced pepper flavour is desired.

Black Pepper works naturally across Indian and global cuisines. Its combination of warmth, aroma and subtle pungency makes it an essential pantry spice, whether used on its own or alongside other spices in traditional blends and seasonings.
