Clove

🌿 Rich Aromatic Flavour

Origin: Kerala

Dharoahar Clove is selected for its rich aroma, distinctive warmth and naturally intense flavour. A classic whole spice, cloves bring depth and character to traditional Indian cooking while also complementing a wide variety of global cuisines.

The whole dried flower buds release their characteristic aroma gradually during cooking, making them ideal for tempering, gravies, curries, rice preparations, marinades and slow-cooked dishes. They can also be combined with other whole spices to create aromatic and flavourful spice blends.

With its warm, slightly sweet and intensely aromatic profile, Clove is particularly valued in dishes where a small amount of spice can add significant depth. From everyday meals to festive preparations, it remains an essential whole spice for a well-stocked kitchen.

  • Origin: Kerala
