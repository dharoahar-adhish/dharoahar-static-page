Kashmiri Mirch

🌿 Deep Red Elegance

Origin: Guntur

Dharoahar Kashmiri Mirch is a finely ground chilli powder known for its rich red colour, smooth texture and mild, pleasant warmth. It is especially valued in Indian cooking when a dish needs vibrant colour along with a balanced chilli character.

Kashmiri Mirch is a versatile ingredient for curries, gravies, marinades and traditional Indian preparations. Its vivid colour helps create an appealing finish, while its comparatively mild heat allows the other ingredients and spices in a dish to remain prominent.

The fine powder blends easily into sauces, spice mixes and marinades, making it convenient for both everyday cooking and more elaborate preparations. It can be used to build colour and flavour gradually or combined with other spices to create a richer and more layered seasoning profile.
